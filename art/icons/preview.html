Hero Football Manager — App Icon Concepts

Round 2 — genuine NES/SNES style: three 32×32 hand-authored pixel maps (rows-of-chars grids, no drawing primitives), each ≤16 colors with hard black outlines and discrete 2–4 step shading ramps, nearest-neighbor upscaled to 1024px (App Store), 180px (@3x home screen), and 120px (@2x home screen). Rendered on a dark wallpaper mock with the iOS superellipse corner approximated at 22.5% border-radius. The "Hero FC" label is page furniture for this mock only — it is not part of the icon file.

Chunky outlined soccer ball blazing in from the upper-left on a vertically striped pitch. Discrete 4-step flame ramp (red → orange → gold → pale core), hard black outlines, single-pixel gold sparks. 9 colors.

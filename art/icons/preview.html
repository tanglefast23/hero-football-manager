Hero Football Manager — App Icon Concepts

Round 3 — ball-centric trio, no fire: three 32×32 hand-authored pixel maps (rows-of-chars grids, no drawing primitives), each ≤16 colors with hard black outlines and discrete 2–3 step shading ramps, nearest-neighbor upscaled to 1024px (App Store), 180px (@3x home screen), and 120px (@2x home screen). Rendered on a dark wallpaper mock with the iOS superellipse corner approximated at 22.5% border-radius. The "Hero FC" label is page furniture for this mock only — it is not part of the icon file.
Concept A — Caped Ball no text
The ball IS the hero: chunky outlined soccer ball wearing a red cape, knotted at the top-left rim, billowing off to the left with a pointed trailing edge. 3-step cape ramp, navy background, gold sparks. 8 colors.

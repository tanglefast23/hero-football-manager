# M4 Content & Polish Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Deliver the complete M4 roadmap on top of the accepted M3 working tree, while closing repository gaps that prevent M4 from being truthful or testable.

**Architecture:** Keep `src/sim/` and `src/game/` deterministic pure TypeScript. Store authored game content in typed JSON. Put settings in the existing schema-versioned preferences repository, career outcomes in the existing save codec, and presentation-only art/audio/haptics in UI/render modules. Any replay-affecting power change must bump `ENGINE_VERSION` and regenerate/inspect both golden replay artifacts.

**Tech stack:** Expo/React Native, TypeScript, Zustand, Zod, Jest, Skia, expo-audio, expo-haptics, SQLite preferences/career persistence.

## Scope and acceptance map

1. **Accepted M3 baseline**
   - Preserve and include the current potential-rating changes.
   - Record Joe's M3 gate acceptance in `docs/10-roadmap.md`.
   - Verify focused M3 tests, TypeScript, and the full Jest suite before the M4 commit series.
2. **M3 prerequisite gaps discovered during M4 reconnaissance**
   - Repository currently has 3 implemented powers, not the roadmap's 12.
   - Repository currently has 1 chance event, not M3's ~15 or M4's ~30.

3. **30-event launch catalog**
   - Expand `content/events.json` to ~30 authored events across every canonical category.
   - Extend Zod triggers/requirements for divisions, facilities, personality, money, player selection, repeatability/chains, and success presentation metadata.

4. **Risky-success story cutscenes**
   - Persist enough resolved-outcome identity to distinguish success/failure and rewards.

5. **Portrait paper doll and customization**
   - Add typed, persisted appearance choices for the created player (skin, hair, kit/accent, accessory/body silhouette as feasible without changing balance).

   - Render/cache composed portrait layers with nearest-neighbour pixel sampling and use them on player-facing screens.
6. **Comic cut-in art pass**
   - Create a launch set of generated comic/pixel artwork and/or deterministic in-app comic compositions for all shipped power and event-success moments.
   - Add readable power name/player banner, hero/rival palette, first-view timing, later tap-to-skip behavior, and Reduce Motion banner-only behavior.
7. **Sound and haptics**

8. **Tutorial/onboarding fiction**

9. **Accessibility settings**
   - Persist Reduce Motion, HUD side, haptics, text scale, high contrast/color-safe kits, and cut-in behavior.
   - Ensure 44pt targets, labels/roles/states, non-color selection cues, dynamic text on management screens, and no red-vs-green kits.
10. **Cozy / Chairman difficulty**

14. **Verification and audit loop**

    - Use computer control to exercise every reasonably reachable M4 gate: creation customization, both difficulties, event safe/risky outcomes, success cutscene, settings persistence, text/reduce motion/HUD/haptics controls, cut-ins, awards/recap, save/relaunch continuity, and compact-screen layout.

## Risks and controls

- **Scope size:** split into independently verified slices; no broad untested rewrite.
- **Replay drift:** version decision before changing power behavior; inspect rather than blindly update goldens.
- **Save compatibility:** new career fields optional/defaulted and codec-tested with legacy fixtures.
- **Art inconsistency:** follow docs/11 palette, pixel grid, multicultural cast rules, and nearest-neighbour rendering.
- **Balance runtime:** keep focused gates separate from expensive soak suites and run long suites in tracked background processes.
- **Existing dirty work:** never reset/stash/discard; commit accepted M3 baseline first after verification.

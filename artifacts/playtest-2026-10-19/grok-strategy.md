The strongest Chairman plan is to buy weekly training points and two starters first, and to send a scout only when the slip lands inside a signing window. The last run's facility tour, backup-keeper drills, and Week 1 four-week mission are the things to drop.

**Code facts**

- Chairman takes a 40% Season 1 wage subsidy, 80% sponsor income, and one $10,000 emergency loan. Rivals gain 5% on all seven attributes each season. Cozy's numbers are 50%, 100%, $20,000, and 2.5%.
- A club with no buildings gets 10 training points a week. A Level 1 Training Pitch adds 12. Each further pitch level adds another 12. The pitch costs $8,000 and takes two weeks. Its first upgrade costs $20,000 for the same 12 points.
- A Level 1 head coach adds 5 points a week at $300. He does not need an office. A Level 1 assistant adds 3 points, needs the Coaching Office, and costs about $150. The office is $6,500 and takes one week.
- Upgrading that office to Level 2 before the hire adds one level. For a stored Level 1 head, that is one extra point a week and a wage of $600 instead of $300. The stored Level 1 assistant stays at 3 points and goes from about $150 to $300. Hire both before that upgrade.
- Outfield Tier 1 is 7 points for +3 stored. Tier 4 is the best raw return, 26 points for +13. Tier 5 is 39 for +18. Keeper drills use the same point costs for +1, +2, +4, +7, and +10 stored reflexes. A goalkeeper's card adds a display-only bonus on top. The match, the valuation, and the lineup use the stored reflex. Shooting, defending, and technique do nothing for a goalkeeper. Reflexes do nothing for an outfielder.
- Gym, Keeper Court, Shooting Range, and Tech Center add a 10% drill multiplier at Level 1. They do not add weekly points. Defer them.
- A new Fan Shop is $5,000 and one week. A new Stand is $10,000 and three weeks. Each new building adds a full commercial step. The first upgrade adds half a step and costs $15,000 or $25,000. One shop beside one stand later adds 10% merchandise.
- Registration is Weeks 1–4 and 17–18. A mission lasts 2 or 3 weeks, or 3 or 4 if the brief names a role. It resolves on its due week. A report that arrives in Week 5 cannot be signed until Week 17. The slip shows the exact length before you pay. A role brief is worth sending in Week 1 only when that slip says 3 weeks. The Scout Office is $6,000. Level 0 returns 2 names. Level 1 returns 3 names and a wider attribute range. The office level is fixed when the mission starts.
- Immediate Starter ranks players by how far they are above your current starter at that role, then shuffles a band. It does not hand you the single best player. Local with no named role costs $2,000. Naming the role adds $750 and the extra week.
- The Week 19 trip sets the training-point bank to 0, takes 10 condition off every player, and adds +1 to all seven stored stats at D5, or +2 at D4. Green Bull is not offered in D5 or D4. From D3 it costs $50,000 for +2 and it also empties the bank.
- Negotiating a renewal lands below the asking wage. Signing at the ask pays the whole ask and attaches no promise. A Starter or Captain promise claims a lineup place. Training Priority claims the next drills. Hero License is 2 until D3, then 3, then 4 at D1. A bought permit replaces that cap. It does not add to it.
- The old unlicensed-hero crash is fixed. The buyer skips an unlicensed powered player. Selling one still fails if he is the only available player of that role.
- Academy fees are $500, $750, $1,000, and $1,250 by Youth Field level. A sale uses the normal valuation. One prospect in the last run sold for $5,776. That price is not a rule.

**The run**

Hire the cheap head in Week 1. Start the Training Pitch. Build the Level 1 Coaching Office and hire the cheap assistant before any office upgrade. Build the Level 1 Scout Office before the first mission.

In Week 1, send a local Immediate Starter for a goalkeeper or a forward only if the slip is due by Week 4. If the role brief says 4 weeks, send the unnamed brief instead, or hold the role brief for a start week that lands on Week 17 or 18. Spend the fee on a starting goalkeeper and a quick forward before any support building. Read stored reflexes, or the scout range, not the card.

Put points into the starting eleven's live stats. Pace and stamina are live for defenders and midfielders. Do not drill the backup goalkeeper, and do not buy reflexes on outfielders. Spend a bank down before Week 19. Take that trip only when little is left, because it deletes the whole bank.

Add Level 1 shops and one stand after the eleven is funded. Leave the Youth Field, medical pair, gym pair, ranges, and courts for later. Keep more than one week of wages, because the only loan is $10,000.

Use 4-4-2 until a third forward is actually strong. The last run's 7–0 and its small energy sample were one squad. They do not pick the tactic.

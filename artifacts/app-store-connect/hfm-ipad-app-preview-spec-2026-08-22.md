# Hero Football Manager — iPad App Preview Specification

## Deliverable

- One portrait iPad App Preview for the English (U.S.) product page.
- File name: `HFM-iPad-App-Preview-01-English-US.mp4`
- Target length: 29 seconds. Apple accepts 15–30 seconds.
- Final size: 1200 × 1600 pixels.
- Format: H.264 `.mp4`.
- Frame rate: 30 FPS maximum, progressive scan.
- Target video bit rate: 10–12 Mbps.
- Audio: AAC stereo, 256 kbps, 48 kHz.
- Maximum file size: 500 MB.

Record the native portrait iPad layout at 2064 × 2752 or 2048 × 2732. Scale the finished edit to 1200 × 1600. Do not stretch or crop away game controls.

## 29-second edit

| Time | Footage | Overlay text |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:04 | Start inside a live match. Show the strongest hero-power activation and its result. | `HEROES CHANGE MATCHES` |
| 0:04–0:09 | Show live coaching. Change Formation, Playstyle, and Energy Use. Use small touch markers if needed. | `COACH EVERY MOMENT` |
| 0:09–0:14 | Show a player’s six stats, then a training choice or completed drill. | `TRAIN WHAT MATTERS` |
| 0:14–0:19 | Show the facility grid with several buildings and a visible adjacency bonus. | `BUILD THE WHOLE CLUB` |
| 0:19–0:24 | Show one meaningful story, player request, or contract-negotiation choice. The choice and consequence must be readable. | `CHOICES HAVE CONSEQUENCES` |
| 0:24–0:29 | Show a filled Hero Cup bracket, then finish on a strong in-game club or victory screen. | `CLIMB 5 DIVISIONS + CUP` |

Use quick straight cuts or 0.15–0.25 second dissolves. Do not use a separate logo card. Keep real gameplay visible throughout.

## Visual rules

- Use only real footage captured from the submitted game.
- Show the iPad layout, not enlarged iPhone footage.
- Do not show hands, an iPad frame, Simulator controls, debug menus, or editing software.
- Use the approved comic-style title lettering without a box.
- Put overlay text about one quarter down from the top when it does not cover important play.
- Keep text inside an 80-pixel left/right margin and a 100-pixel top/bottom margin.
- Keep each text line visible for at least 2.5 seconds.
- Use simple fades or dissolves. Do not imply features that the game does not have.
- Do not show prices, dates, ratings, real clubs, real players, or third-party brands.
- Use fictional player and club names only.

## Audio rules

- The video must still make sense when muted because App Store previews autoplay muted.
- Use only cleared game music and sound effects.
- Do not use voice-over for the first version. This avoids another localization requirement.
- Keep music consistent across cuts. Keep sound effects below the music peak.

## Poster frame

Choose a clean hero-power frame from the first four seconds. It must show the pitch, players, and comic power effect without covering the score or important UI.

## Acceptance check

- Duration is between 15 and 30 seconds.
- Resolution is exactly 1200 × 1600.
- Frame rate is no more than 30 FPS.
- The file is below 500 MB.
- Every scene is captured from the current submitted build.
- No debug UI, cursor, taps from the simulator, personal information, or unlicensed content appears.
- All overlay text is readable when viewed at normal iPad App Store size.
- The preview remains understandable with audio muted.

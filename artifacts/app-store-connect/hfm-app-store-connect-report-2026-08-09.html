In the app

Seven languages are configured

CFBundleLocalizations and the in-game language registry both list en, es, pt-BR, fr, de, id, vi. The game chooses by the device language, not the player’s country.

Example: a Vietnamese-speaking player in Canada can get Vietnamese. A Spanish-speaking player in the United States can get Spanish.

On the App Store

English (U.S.) only, for now

Apple does not translate the subtitle, description, keywords, or screenshots from the binary. With only English metadata, Apple uses English as the fallback in storefronts without another matching localization.

This is valid for version 1.0 while the game and marketing package are unfinished.

Description

Build a small football club into a global force—one week, one decision, and one impossible comeback at a time.

Hero Football Manager is a cozy, single-player club-management game where ordinary footballers can awaken extraordinary powers.

BUILD THE WHOLE CLUB
• Train players and shape their development.
• Recruit staff, scout the market, and manage contracts.
• Upgrade your ground and facilities.
• Balance wages, sponsorships, match income, and long-term growth.
• Guide a fictional club through five divisions and the Hero Cup.

WATCH EVERY PLAN PLAY OUT
Matches unfold in lively pixel art. Adjust your formation, playstyle, substitutions, and energy use while your team plays. Heroes build toward automatic, context-sensitive powers that can turn a match into a comic-book spectacle.

A COMPLETE, PLAYER-FIRST GAME
• One purchase. No ads or in-app purchases.
• No account, analytics, or tracking.
• Designed for offline play.
• Your career stays on your device.
• Seven languages: English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Indonesian, and Vietnamese.

Build the club. Discover your heroes. Climb from the district leagues to the global stage.

App Review notes

Hero Football Manager is a paid, single-player football club-management game. No account or sign-in is required. The game has no ads or in-app purchases and is designed to work offline. All clubs and players are fictional, and career progress is stored on the device.

The game starts with character creation and a guided first week. Watched matches play automatically. During a match, the player can adjust Formation, Playstyle, substitutions, and Energy Use. Superpowers activate automatically when their match context occurs.

No reviewer account, demo credentials, permission grant, or special access is required.

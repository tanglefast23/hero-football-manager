Description
Run a small football club where ordinary players can wake up with superpowers.

Hero Football Manager is a single-player club management game. You train the squad, hire staff, upgrade the ground, and keep the books. Then you watch that plan play out in pixel-art matches.

BUILD THE CLUB
• Train players and shape how they grow.
• Scout the market, hire staff, and handle contracts.
• Upgrade your ground and facilities.
• Climb five divisions and the Hero Cup.

WATCH THE MATCH
Change formation, playstyle, substitutions, and energy while the team plays. Heroes trigger powers on their own when the match context fits.

ONE PURCHASE
No ads or in-app purchases. No account, analytics, or tracking. Play offline. Your career stays on your device.

English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Indonesian, and Vietnamese.
App Review notes
Hero Football Manager is a paid, single-player football club-management game. No account or sign-in is required. The game has no ads or in-app purchases and is designed to work offline. All clubs and players are fictional, and career progress is stored on the device. The game starts with character creation and a guided first week. Watched matches play automatically. During a match, the player can adjust Formation, Playstyle, substitutions, and Energy Use. Superpowers activate automatically when their match context occurs. No reviewer account, demo credentials, permission grant, or special access is required.

# Hero Football Manager — Indie Advertising Playbook

Date: 22 August 2026  
Product: US$3.99 paid iPhone and iPad game  
Planning proceeds: about US$2.80 per U.S. sale  
Core promise: superpowered football management, one purchase, no ads, no in-app purchases, offline play

## The decision in one page

Do not try to win by buying broad awareness.

The game earns about $2.80 from one U.S. sale. There is no later ad or in-app purchase revenue. Paid marketing must therefore stay small and measurable.

Use this order:

1. Release with the strongest App Store page possible.
2. Run small Apple Ads search tests for people already looking for games.
3. Post short gameplay clips on TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.
4. Spend TikTok credits only on an organic clip that already performed well.
5. Give promo codes to suitable small creators.
6. Submit an Apple featuring nomination.
7. Localize the store page before buying non-English traffic.

Reddit and enthusiast forums are not core channels. Do not spend time trying to win arguments there.

## What success means

The planning ceiling is **$2.80 cost per purchase**. Treat this as a ceiling, not a target.

- **Scale:** CPA is $1.75 or less after at least 10 paid purchases.
- **Hold and learn:** CPA is $1.76–$2.40.
- **Pause:** CPA is above $2.80.
- **Stop the whole paid channel:** its spend exceeds its attributed proceeds for two straight weeks.

CPA means cost per acquired buyer. For this paid game, one first-time download is one purchase.

Do not judge success from views, likes, or angry comments. Judge it from purchases, store-page conversion, and money returned.

## Positioning

Lead with the game, not the development method.

Use these four messages repeatedly:

1. **Ordinary players wake up with superpowers.**
2. **Run the whole club, then watch the plan play out.**
3. **Pay once. No ads. No in-app purchases.**
4. **Play offline. Your career stays on your device.**

The first three App Store screenshots already form a useful sales sequence:

1. Heroes Change Matches — the unique hook.
2. Contract Renewal — management depth.
3. Coach Live — control during matches.

Do not keep changing the store set during launch week. First collect real conversion data.

## Channel priority

### 1. App Store conversion

This is the highest-value work. Every channel sends people here.

Before release:

- Confirm both App Preview videos have finished processing.
- Confirm all ten iPhone and ten iPad screenshots are present and ordered.
- Confirm the Caped Ball icon appears with the uploaded build.
- Keep the short description focused on superpowers, club management, and one-purchase value.
- Submit an Apple featuring nomination. Emphasize the unusual sports-superhero mix, offline play, seven in-game languages, no tracking, and solo-developer story.

Apple lets developers nominate launches and major updates for editorial consideration. A nomination costs nothing and does not guarantee placement.

### 2. Apple Ads search results

Apple Ads reaches people while they search the App Store. It is the best first paid channel because intent is high and Apple reports downloads directly.

Start with one U.S. search-results campaign:

- Daily budget: **$5**.
- Duration: **7 days**, then review.
- Strategy: Manage Bids.
- Match type: Exact match.
- Search Match: Off for the first test.
- CPA cap: **$2.40** if the interface permits it.

Start with these descriptive terms:

- hero football manager
- football manager game
- soccer manager game
- club management game
- pixel football game
- offline sports game
- soccer simulation

Do not use real club names, player names, or another game's trademark in ad text or store metadata.

Apple Ads rules:

- Pause a keyword after **$15 spend and zero purchases**.
- Pause a keyword when its 7-day CPA is above **$2.80**.
- Keep a keyword unchanged while it is below **$2.40** but has fewer than 10 purchases.
- Raise its budget by at most **25% each week** when CPA stays at or below **$1.75**.
- Add broad-match discovery only after exact-match terms produce purchases.

Apple Ads has no minimum spend. Its daily budget controls average daily spending, although an individual day can exceed that average. The monthly total is capped at the daily budget multiplied by 30.4.

### 3. Organic TikTok, Reels, and Shorts

Use one clean 9:16 export for all three platforms. Do not repost a version with another platform's watermark.

The App Store preview is source material, not the final social post. Cut it into short scenes.

Create nine clips across three hook groups:

**A. Superpower spectacle**

- “Your goalkeeper just became a giant.”
- “My striker literally caught fire.”
- “Football tactics, until this happens.”

**B. Management depth**

- “I promoted this tiny club with one terrible budget.”
- “This hero wants five times his old wage.”
- “One bad promise can lose the dressing room.”

**C. Honest premium value**

- “A mobile game with no ads or in-app purchases.”
- “Pay once. Build the club. Play offline.”
- “Seven languages. One career stored on your phone.”

Each video should:

- Show movement or a power in the first second.
- Run for about 9–20 seconds.
- Use large captions that work with sound off.
- Show real gameplay, not only title cards.
- End with “Hero Football Manager — App Store — $3.99.”
- Use one clear call to action.

Post three times each week. Reuse the same footage with a different first line before recording more footage.

Organic test rule:

- After 48 hours, rank posts by watch completion, shares, saves, profile visits, and store-link clicks.
- Drop a hook after three versions all perform below your account's normal result.
- Make two new versions of any hook that performs at least twice your normal result.

### 4. TikTok credits

Do not start with an iOS App Promotion campaign. TikTok recommends a Mobile Measurement Partner for app marketing. Without one, TikTok recommends the **Traffic** objective and click measurement. This protects the game's “Data Not Collected” position and avoids adding a tracking SDK only for advertising.

Use this first paid test:

- Objective: Traffic.
- Destination: the App Store campaign link when Apple makes it available.
- One campaign.
- One ad group.
- Three ads using the three best organic hooks.
- U.S. only for the first test.
- Age: 18–44.
- Duration: 3 days.
- Ad-group budget: just over TikTok's required **$20 daily minimum**.
- Use credits first.

TikTok requires campaign budgets above $50 when a campaign-level budget is used. Ad-group daily budgets must exceed $20. Keep the structure small so the minimum does not multiply across several ad groups.

Use Spark Ads to boost the existing winning post. Engagement then remains on the organic post.

Early stop rules:

- Pause an ad after 3,000 impressions when link CTR is below **0.8%**.
- Keep testing when link CTR is **0.8%–1.49%**.
- A link CTR of **1.5% or more** earns a second creative version.
- Pause the test after **$25–$30 spend with no visible purchase lift**.
- Never add cash after the credits unless estimated CPA is below **$2.40**.

These TikTok thresholds are working rules, not platform guarantees. App Store campaign reporting can hide small samples. Compare the paid period with the prior three-day sales baseline.

### 5. Small creators

Target creators with roughly 5,000–100,000 followers in these groups:

- Premium mobile games.
- Pixel and retro games.
- Football and soccer games.
- Cozy management games.
- “Games worth buying” recommendations.

Send ten personal messages each week. Use this structure:

> Hi [name]. Your video about [specific game/topic] made me think you might enjoy Hero Football Manager. It is a $3.99 offline club-management game where ordinary players develop superpowers. I can send an App Store promo code and a clean gameplay pack. There is no obligation to post or review it positively.

Apple permits up to 100 promo codes per app version. Codes last four weeks and work once. A player who uses a promo code cannot rate or review the app, so use codes for coverage, not ratings.

Do not demand a script, approval rights, or silence about AI. Ask creators to disclose that the game was gifted.

Pay for a creator only after their unpaid or comparable work proves audience fit. A small paid test should have one creator, one deliverable, usage rights in writing, and a maximum budget set before contact.

### 6. Press and free distribution

Build one small press-kit page containing:

- A one-sentence hook.
- Price and platforms.
- Caped Ball icon.
- Five best screenshots.
- iPhone vertical video.
- iPad landscape video.
- Feature list.
- Contact email.
- Promo-code availability.

Email no more than 10–15 well-matched mobile-game writers or newsletters at launch. Personalize one sentence. Do not send a mass attachment.

Also use:

- Apple featuring nomination.
- Your own website.
- TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
- A short email list if you already have one.
- Release notes and update announcements after launch.

Skip Product Hunt, broad display ads, Facebook awareness campaigns, and gaming forums during the first month. They add work before the core channels are measured.

## Measurement without a tracking SDK

Use:

1. App Store Connect Analytics.
2. Apple Ads reporting.
3. Apple campaign links.
4. Promo-code redemption counts.
5. A simple weekly spreadsheet.

Apple campaign links can report product-page views, first-time downloads, sales, territory, and device. A new app may need at least 24 hours of analytics data before links become available. Campaign metrics appear only after the privacy threshold is met, including at least five first-time downloads for download data.

Track these columns:

| Date | Channel | Creative | Country | Spend | Store clicks | Product views | Purchases | Proceeds | CPA | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

Run one 48-hour no-paid-ad period every third week. Compare its normal sales with paid periods. This helps reveal whether advertising creates new sales or only takes credit for buyers who would have purchased anyway.

## Store localization rule

The game contains seven languages, but the current store page has only English (U.S.) metadata.

Apple displays the English metadata when no matching store localization exists. The binary translation does not translate the App Store description or keywords.

Therefore:

- Start paid ads in the U.S.
- Add the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Ireland, and New Zealand only after checking the page and price in each storefront.
- Localize Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, and German store metadata before paid ads in those languages.
- Add Indonesian after the first four localizations.
- Do not target Vietnam or mainland China because you chose not to offer version 1.0 there.

## AI questions and hostile comments

Do not make AI the advertising message. Do not conceal it or claim the game is “100% handmade” if that is not true.

Use one answer:

> I am a solo developer and I use modern tools in my workflow. I make the design, balance, and release decisions, and I test the finished game. I am happy to answer questions about how it plays.

Then return to the game.

- Answer a civil question once.
- Do not debate production methods in a long thread.
- Do not use alternate accounts.
- Do not ask fans to attack critics.
- Hide threats, slurs, spam, and repeated abuse.
- Leave fair criticism visible.
- Do not advertise in a community that repeatedly becomes hostile.

TikTok requires labels for realistic AI-generated or significantly AI-edited content. Normal captured gameplay does not need an AI label merely because AI tools were used during development. Label an ad when its actual video, voice, or realistic imagery meets TikTok's AI-content rule.

## Music rule

App Store clearance does not automatically prove paid-social clearance.

For TikTok ads, use:

- Game audio that is expressly cleared for paid advertising, or
- TikTok's Commercial Music Library.

The Commercial Music Library contains music cleared for business use. Do not use a trending personal-account sound unless its commercial rights are clear.

## Weekly schedule after launch

Keep marketing under six hours each week.

| Day | Time | Work |
|---|---:|---|
| Monday | 45 min | Record sales, CPA, and store conversion. Apply stop rules. |
| Tuesday | 75 min | Cut three short videos from existing footage. |
| Wednesday | 45 min | Post video 1. Send five creator messages. |
| Thursday | 45 min | Check Apple Ads terms and add negative keywords. |
| Friday | 45 min | Post video 2. Reply to reviews and useful comments. |
| Saturday | 20 min | Post video 3. Send five creator messages. |
| Sunday | 0 min | Stop. Protect development time. |

## Budget tiers

### Tier 0 — credits and free work

- TikTok credits only.
- Organic short videos.
- Apple featuring nomination.
- Promo-code outreach.
- Press kit and direct outreach.

Use this tier until the app is live and conversion data exists.

### Tier 1 — $150 for the first month

- $105: Apple Ads at $5 daily for 21 days.
- Up to $45: one extra Apple Ads or creator test.
- TikTok remains credits-only.

Stay here unless a channel holds CPA at or below $1.75 for two weeks.

### Tier 2 — up to $400 monthly

Enter only after Tier 1 works.

- 60% Apple Ads winners.
- 25% proven TikTok creative.
- 15% one small creator test.

Return to Tier 0 if blended paid CPA exceeds $2.80 for two weeks.

Do not use a larger tier until the game has at least 50 paid-attributed purchases and two weeks below $1.75 CPA.

## 30-day launch checklist

### Before release

- [ ] Attach and validate the App Store build.
- [ ] Confirm both videos and both ten-screenshot sets.
- [ ] Confirm the Caped Ball icon comes from the build.
- [ ] Submit the Apple featuring nomination.
- [ ] Prepare nine short vertical clips.
- [ ] Prepare the press-kit page.
- [ ] Create a list of 40 suitable creators.
- [ ] Draft one U.S. Apple Ads campaign but leave it paused.
- [ ] Confirm paid-social rights for every sound used.

### Days 1–3

- [ ] Release manually after Apple approval.
- [ ] Verify the public U.S. page on an iPhone and iPad.
- [ ] Start Apple Ads at $5 daily.
- [ ] Post one power clip on TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.
- [ ] Send ten creator messages with promo-code offers.
- [ ] Record daily units and proceeds.

### Days 4–7

- [ ] Post two more organic clips.
- [ ] Choose the strongest organic hook.
- [ ] Start one three-day TikTok Traffic test with credits.
- [ ] Email 10–15 suitable press contacts.
- [ ] Pause any Apple keyword that crosses its stop rule.
- [ ] Do not change store screenshots yet.

### Days 8–14

- [ ] Make two variants of the best hook.
- [ ] Send ten more creator messages.
- [ ] Review product-page conversion and CPA.
- [ ] Stop paid TikTok if there is no sales lift.
- [ ] Keep only Apple keywords below the planning ceiling.

### Days 15–21

- [ ] Pause paid ads for 48 hours and record the baseline.
- [ ] Compare paid and unpaid sales.
- [ ] Start Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, and German store localizations.
- [ ] Prepare a real update or useful patch announcement.

### Days 22–30

- [ ] Pick Tier 0, Tier 1, or Tier 2 from measured CPA.
- [ ] Keep three organic posts each week.
- [ ] Stop channels that exceed proceeds.
- [ ] Submit another featuring nomination only for a meaningful update.
- [ ] Write the next 30-day plan from actual sales, not views.

## Council Audit

All three independent reviewers completed successfully with the same scope.

- Claude Fable 5, xhigh: prioritized App Store conversion, featuring, organic TikTok, and creator outreach.
- Claude Opus 5, xhigh: applied the strictest CPA gates and identified English-only store metadata as a paid-traffic risk.
- Grok 4.6, high: prioritized privacy, no tracking SDK, Traffic-objective TikTok tests, Apple Ads, and a small press list.

Final synthesis:

- **3/3 agreement:** paid acquisition is dangerous at $2.80 proceeds per sale.
- **3/3 agreement:** App Store conversion comes before scaling ads.
- **3/3 agreement:** Apple Ads is the best first cash channel.
- **3/3 agreement:** TikTok should start with organic creative and credits.
- **3/3 agreement:** do not add a tracking SDK only for marketing.
- **3/3 agreement:** creator outreach should use promo codes and no editorial demands.
- **3/3 agreement:** Reddit and hostile enthusiast forums should not be core channels.
- **1/3 explicit finding, independently confirmed:** English-only store metadata can waste non-English paid traffic.

The council's suggested budgets were adjusted to match TikTok's current minimums. Claims that required current platform facts were checked against Apple and TikTok documentation.

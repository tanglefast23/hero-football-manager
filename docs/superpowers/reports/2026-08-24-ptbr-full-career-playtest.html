Fix first

Critical economy

A maxed stat still accepts a paid drill

Sam Mitts had 999 Reflexes. The goalkeeper drill still promised +20, charged 39 TP, and then awarded +0.

Fix: disable the drill at 999. Near the cap, show the exact gain before payment.

High balance

Early Cup rounds are not competitive

The final run won 12–0 in the round of 32 and 16–0 in the round of 16. These matches add time but no decision or tension.

Fix: from D2 onward, draw only D3-or-stronger clubs. Keep later D1 rounds difficult.

High state bug

Transfer requests can stay after morale recovers

Dara Moss rose from 25 to 45 morale after a gift. Her transfer request stayed visible that week and the next week.

Fix: re-evaluate the request after a morale change and explain whether it ended or remains active.

High lineup

License changes can leave a weak replacement

Milo Dunn awakened and became unlicensed. Ivo Ward, overall 74, replaced him. After licenses changed, the weak replacement could remain until the correct hero became eligible.

Fix: rebuild only the affected role. Choose the best eligible natural-role player and preserve all valid starters.

Copy and identity

Two starters still share shirt number 10

Gio Gray and Léo Costa both appeared as number 10 in the same lineup.

Fix: assign unique active-squad shirt numbers after signings, season resets, and lineup changes.

Content repetition

The same request repeats too often

Ele pediu por escrito appeared repeatedly across Seasons 8–10, sometimes close to another request. It became predictable.

Fix: add a season cooldown for the exact request and weight unseen requests higher.

Scouting

Potential grades can disagree

Milo Dunn showed C− in a scout report but B− after joining the club.

Fix: use one potential-grade function for reports, Deals, and player files.

Contract flow

A starter promise can block a valid offer

One negotiation returned “not possible” with a starter promise. A captain promise worked. Direct full-ask renewals remained safe.

Fix: validate the selected promise before submission and name the exact conflict.

Whole journey

Ten seasons was long but understandable. The final three D1 seasons gave clear evidence that defense and license depth were the missing pieces.

Optimal strategy used

  • TP: spend useful TP every week. Alternate one-drill and two-drill weeks as the 60-point income crosses the 39-point drill cost.
  • Drills: max every drill path. In D1, focus the three starting defenders after shooting, passing, and goalkeeper stats reach 999.
  • Green Bull: use it before individual drills. Pay only when at least 60 TP is available and a wage reserve remains.
  • Facilities: build three Level 3 stands and three Level 3 shops before miscellaneous facilities. Then max useful training, medical, academy, and lodging buildings.
  • Licenses: buy the seventh permit when Milo awakens. Replacing a weak 74-overall midfielder was worth more than one extra Green Bull trip.
  • Contracts: renew key players for three seasons at full asking wage when a promise conflict could endanger the squad.
  • Cash: do not hoard it. Keep wages and renewals covered, then buy useful facilities, licenses, or Green Bull training.
  • Matches: Quick Result was sufficient for the entire successful title season.

Recommended change order

  1. Stop drills from charging TP when the selected stat is already 999.
  2. Refresh or close transfer requests immediately after morale changes.
  3. Strengthen Cup seeding from D2 onward.
  4. Harden automatic lineup selection after Hero License changes.
  5. Use one potential-grade function across scouting and player files.
  6. Fix the remaining Portuguese fallback labels and duplicate shirt numbers.
  7. Add cooldowns to repeated player requests.

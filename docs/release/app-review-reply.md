3. FUNCTIONS AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Hero Football Manager is a paid, single-player football (soccer) club-management game with a fictional superpower twist. The player creates a club, signs and trains fictional players, manages finances and facilities, and watches matches play out automatically.
It is made for fans of turn-based management sims who want a complete, self-contained club career on a phone, in sessions of a few minutes. The value is a full career with no live service, no timers, no ads and no spending after the initial purchase.

4. SETUP AND ACCESS INSTRUCTIONS
No credentials, demo account, sample files or special access are required.
- Launch the app.
- Create your manager and club in the guided character creation.
- Follow the guided first week (squad, training, inbox, training pitch).
- Tap a fixture to watch a match. Matches play automatically in 3-4 minutes.
- In-match you can change Formation, Playstyle and Energy Use, and make substitutions. Superpowers fire automatically when their in-match context occurs.
Progress is stored on-device in a local SQLite save. Deleting the app deletes the data.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS

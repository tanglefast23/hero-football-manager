## What's New

Choose cheaper training tiers after upgrading, with clear TP, gain and condition previews.

Scout missions now show their exact return week and signing-window fit.

The optional Week 19 training trip preserves older TP savings.

Cup shootouts now use the players and keeper on the pitch at full time. Kicks are saved, so reloading cannot change the result.

Fixed team-sheet positions, season-start lineup changes, Hero License issues and transferred heroes losing their powers. Improved desktop graphics stability.

# App Review screen recording — shot list

Target: 3-4 minutes, portrait, one take, no editing.
Device: iPhone 16 Pro Max, iOS 26.6.
Build: TestFlight build 2 (NOT the Metro dev build).

## Before you start

- Turn on Do Not Disturb. A notification banner in the video looks unprofessional.
- Delete any existing save so the recording starts at character creation.
- Do NOT shake the phone at any point. That opens the dev menu on a dev build.
- Sound on is fine and helps.

## Record

1. Home screen, app not running. Start Screen Recording. Wait 2 seconds.
2. Tap the Hero Football Manager icon. Let the splash and "Opening club
   files..." finish. Do not skip them - Apple wants to see a clean launch.
3. Character creation: choose your manager look, manager name, club name and
   club colours. About 30 seconds.
4. Guided first week: follow the assistant objective line on screen. It walks
   you through, in its own order:
   - "Open squad."
   - "Tap + on a player and train a stat."
   - "Return home."
   - "Check your inbox."
   - "Build your training pitch."
   - "Advance week."
5. Advance weeks until the first fixture. Tap it and choose to watch the match.
6. During the match, open each control so the reviewer sees them:
   - FORMATION
   - PLAYSTYLE
   - ENERGY USE
   Then make one substitution.
7. Let the match finish. Show the result.
8. The Awakening sequence plays after the final whistle. No player has a
   superpower during the first match - the first hero is created here. Play it
   through: THE HUSH, THE ASCENSION, HERO #1, then tap WATCH EXAMPLE to show
   the power in play. Do not tap SKIP.
9. Return to the office. Stop recording.

## Not in this app - do not look for them

- Account registration, login, account deletion (no accounts, no network)
- Purchase or subscription flows (paid app, 0 in-app purchases)
- User-generated content, reporting, blocking (nothing leaves the device)
- Permission prompts (the app requests no permissions)

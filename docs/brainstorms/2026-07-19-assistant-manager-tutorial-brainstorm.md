# Assistant Manager Tutorial — Bert Rudge

## What we are building

Bert Rudge is the club's long-serving assistant manager: an older, gruff football man who knows the professional game and is quietly territorial about the new manager arriving above him. He is useful first and jealous second. His humor should feel dry and cozy, never cruel or obstructive.

The manager arrives with one undeniable credential: they took a high-school team to the national championship. Bert acknowledges it, but makes clear that a real professional club adds weekly wages, contracts, facilities, training resources and a board that expects the books to work.

## Tutorial cadence

Kairosoft management games commonly use a recurring assistant who interrupts with a very short explanation, names the exact menu route or action, and then leaves the player to perform one task. Systems are introduced when they become relevant rather than through one complete manual dump. Pocket League Story's Assistant Coach routes the player to concrete menu actions, while Game Dev Story's secretary returns to announce important events throughout the game.

References:

- [Pocket League Story walkthrough](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/pocket-league-story-walkthrough/)
- [Game Dev Story gameplay](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Game_Dev_Story/Gameplay)
- [Pocket League Story manual transcript](https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Transcript%3AManual_%28Pocket_League_Story%29)

Hero Football Manager follows that rhythm with four saved milestones:

1. **Office introduction:** Bert establishes the relationship, points out weekly wages, summarizes the bottom navigation, and teaches that Home is the manager's desk. One objective: **Open Squad.**
2. **First Squad visit:** Bert explains one-player-per-drill training and its Money/TP cost. One objective: **Train one player once.**
3. **After training:** a persistent objective sends the player back to Home without another interruption.
4. **Return to Home:** Bert explains Next Assignment, Needs Your Call, and Advance Week. One objective: **Read the desk, then advance one week.**

Nothing is hard-blocked. The guide records completion in the career save, does not replay after reload, and does not introduce contract decisions until a real contract decision is approaching.

## Navigation language

- **Home:** the main desk, today's work and urgent calls.
- **Squad:** pick players and train them.
- **Club:** wages, club books and facilities.
- **League:** table, fixtures and rivals.
- **Market:** visibly present but shuttered until its later unlock.

## Visual direction

Bert uses the pixel-bible **Gaffer** archetype: bald crown, heavy brow, deep-set dot eyes, bulbous nose and moustache. His guide is a cream paper card over the live office, with a blue tutorial accent rather than hero gold. Relevant live UI—the money strip or bottom navigation—receives a blue outline while he points at it. The final page always contains one prominent objective before he leaves.

## Copy guardrails

- Bert may be skeptical of the résumé and protective of the club, but he gives accurate help.
- Jokes target professional-football absurdity, paperwork and the situation—not the player's identity.
- One unfamiliar system per return. Mention future systems only in general terms.
- Body copy uses readable sans; labels and objectives use Silkscreen.

**2. Orphan/missing-key CI cannot work on dynamic keys**
§8 gates 1–2 demand every `t()` key exist and every catalog key be referenced. Catalog design uses `event.<id>.*` and id-derived content keys (§3.1, §3.3). Static analysis cannot see `` t(`event.${id}.body`) ``; pure orphan checks will false-fail or force a hollow allowlist. With `roots: ['<rootDir>/src']` only test discovery is limited — imports from `content/` are fine — but without a declared registry of dynamic key factories, the headline CI gate is unimplementable as written.

**3. Character ceilings are not a layout gate**
§1 sells `ceil(len(en)*expansion)+2` as what stands between translation and clipped tables. Silkscreen advances are per-glyph (`W` 1.0em vs `P` 0.75em in `league-table-columns.ts`); equal character counts are unequal pixels. Creation `w-28`/`w-16`, HUD chips, and buttons have no advance tables. Char CI will green while UI clips. Table snapshots (§8.8) and per-locale short forms help measured columns only; the budget is a voice rule, not a clip proof.

**4. §9 “ok” coverage is process theatre**
Per-string model review + blind back-translation + hash-locked `ok` (§8.2–8.3) is enforceable as paperwork, not quality. A second model will rubber-stamp; glossary substring CI breaks on Spanish/German inflection and compounds. Spec admits no native sign-off (§8.5) yet still CI-blocks ship on verdicts. Keep glossary lock + placeholder/glyph/budget gates; demote model audit and back-translation to sample tooling, not merge blockers.

**5. `src/sim` display English is out of the plan’s explicit fix list**
